Samsung Electronics: Integrated Semiconductor Strategy

November 28, 2024 5 min read Stock Insights Research

Despite 2024 stock underperformance, Samsung Electronics builds differentiated competitiveness in the system semiconductor market through world-first 3nm GAA foundry mass production and integrated AI solutions.

Key Insights

  • World-first 3nm GAA foundry mass production (2022)
  • Foundry + Memory + Packaging integration
  • 20% development time reduction via turnkey service
  • 2nm BSPDN process targeted for 2027

1. Integrated Semiconductor Solutions

Unlike TSMC, Samsung Electronics uniquely possesses memory semiconductor, foundry, and advanced packaging capabilities. This enables customers to significantly reduce development and production timeframes compared to working with separate vendors.

2. World-First 3nm GAA Foundry

Samsung successfully launched world-first 3nm GAA (Gate-All-Around) foundry mass production in 2022. GAA technology, where gates fully surround the channel, delivers significant power efficiency and performance improvements over FinFET.

Samsung GAA Technology

  • Gates fully surround channel
  • Minimized power leakage
  • 2nd-gen 3nm process on track

Legacy FinFET

  • Three-sided contact approach
  • Scaling limitations reached
  • Power leakage challenges

3. Next-Generation 2nm Development

Samsung plans 2nm process (SF2Z) development with BSPDN (Back Side Power Delivery Network) technology by 2027. The company secured Japan's Preferred Networks' 2nm-based AI accelerator order, validating its technological capabilities.

Conclusion

Samsung Electronics builds system semiconductor market competitiveness through world-first 3nm GAA production, integrated AI solutions, and 2nm process development.

While 2024 stock performance suffered from near-term earnings concerns, the differentiated value proposition as an integrated semiconductor company with foundry, memory, and packaging capabilities remains intact.
