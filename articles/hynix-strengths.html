SK Hynix: Dominating the AI Memory Market

November 28, 2024 5 min read Stock Insights Research

SK Hynix has emerged as the most prominent player in the AI semiconductor market in 2024, securing an overwhelming position through world-first HBM3E mass production and exclusive NVIDIA supply agreements.

Key Insights

  • World-first HBM3E mass production (March 2024)
  • Exclusive supplier for NVIDIA Hopper and Blackwell
  • Commanding 90% market share in HBM3
  • 11 trillion KRW revenue from NVIDIA in H1 2024

1. World-First HBM3E Mass Production

SK Hynix began full-scale HBM3E mass production in late March 2024, securing a critical first-mover advantage. HBM3E processes 1.18TB of data per second and is optimized for AI training and inference workloads.

2. Proprietary MR-MUF Packaging Technology

SK Hynix's core competitive advantage lies in its proprietary MR-MUF packaging technology. This approach offers superior thermal dissipation and higher productivity compared to competitors' TC-NCF methods, enabling stable high-volume production.

SK Hynix MR-MUF

  • Superior thermal dissipation
  • High productivity and yield
  • Stable mass production

Competitor TC-NCF

  • Relatively lower thermal performance
  • Production constraints
  • Yield challenges

3. NVIDIA Exclusive Supply Agreement

SK Hynix exclusively supplies HBM3 and HBM3E to NVIDIA's latest GPUs, including the Hopper and next-generation Blackwell series. The company is the only supplier meeting NVIDIA's stringent quality standards.

"In H1 2024, SK Hynix achieved 11 trillion KRW in revenue from exclusive NVIDIA HBM supply, holding a dominant 90% market share in HBM3."

4. Next-Generation Technology Development

SK Hynix continues advancing beyond current production, preparing HBM3E 16-Hi mass production for December 2024. This variant offers up to 18% improved AI training performance and 32% enhanced inference performance compared to 12-Hi.

Conclusion

SK Hynix leads the AI memory market through world-first HBM3E production, proprietary packaging technology, and exclusive NVIDIA supply agreements.

While November saw price correction due to short-term profit-taking, the long-term growth outlook remains highly positive given the 90% HBM market share and sustained AI semiconductor market expansion.
